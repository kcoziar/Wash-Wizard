A real person reads every message. Tell us what you were doing and which iPhone and iOS version you use. Please leave out your customers’ details. Emailing from the app (Settings › Support) adds the app version and your purchase ID for you.

What uses one of my free quotes?

Creating a new quote to send. Drafts, fixing a quote you already made, revisions and sharing again never count.

Why do some numbers have a dashed outline?

They came from the customer's message or an estimate, so they still need your OK. Tap Looks right and the outline turns into a tick.

Can customers see my costs or hourly pay?

No. The quote only shows the services, prices, tax, deposit, terms and your business details. Costs, times, budgets and notes stay private.

Where are my jobs stored?

On your iPhone only. Use Settings › Backup & data › Export backup to save a file to Files or iCloud Drive. Restoring purchases brings back Pro, not jobs.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

On the old iPhone, go to Settings › Backup & data, tap Export backup and save the file to iCloud Drive or Files. On the new iPhone, install the app, open the same screen, tap Choose backup file and pick it. Then tap Restore purchases in Settings to bring back Pro.

I paid for Pro but the app does not show it.

In Settings, tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple account you bought with. If it still does not show, email us from Settings › Support so the email includes your purchase ID.

How do I cancel Pro?

Settings › Manage subscription opens your Apple subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Your jobs and quotes stay.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are billed by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
