The short version

Pressure Washing Calculator+ keeps your jobs, customers, rates, costs and notes on your iPhone. There is no account to create, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics or crash-reporting service. Kozi Publishing LLC does not run a server that receives or stores your quotes or your customers' details.

Who we are

Pressure Washing Calculator+ is published by Kozi Publishing LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. Questions: support@kozipublishing.com.

What stays on your device

Quotes, customer names, addresses, phone numbers and email addresses, request text you paste, rates, visit times, costs, private notes, completion recaps, your business details and logo are stored by the app on your iPhone. Reading a pasted customer message happens on the device; the text is not sent anywhere. We cannot see, access or recover this information.

Device backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, the app's data is included in that backup like other app data, under your Apple account. We do not have access to it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat, a subscription service provider, to check whether Pro is active. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID, purchase and receipt details, and basic device and app information such as the iOS version, app version, country and language. No customer, job, pricing or note data is sent to RevenueCat. RevenueCat’s privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Sending quotes and texts

When you share, text or print a quote, the app hands it to the iPhone share sheet, Messages or the print dialog, and you choose who receives it. The app does not send messages itself and does not read replies. The customer document contains only the scope, prices, tax, deposit, terms and the business details you entered. Your costs, time, margin, budget notes and private notes are never included.

Clipboard, photos and other permissions

The app reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste. If you add a logo, the system photo picker gives the app only the image you select. The app does not request access to your photo library, camera, contacts, microphone or location.

Emails to support

If you email us from the app, the draft includes the app version, iOS version, your Pro status and the random purchase ID so we can find a subscription. You can see and edit the email before sending, and nothing about your customers is added. We use what you send only to answer you and keep it only as long as needed for that.

Analytics and crash reports

The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics under Apple’s own privacy policy.

Backups you export

A backup file contains your jobs, customer details and private costs. You decide where it goes. The app does not encrypt backup files, so store them somewhere you trust.

Deleting your data

Settings › Backup & data › Delete all data removes your jobs, customers, rates, costs, notes and business details from this iPhone. A small anonymous count of free quotes used is kept so that deleting data does not reset it; it contains no customer or job information. Deleting the app removes everything stored by the app. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your Apple account settings.

Your choices and rights

Because your records stay on your device, you can view, change, export and delete them yourself at any time. For questions about the purchase information held by RevenueCat for us, or any other privacy request, email support@kozipublishing.com with the subject "Privacy Request". We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

Children

The app is a business tool for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new date. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
